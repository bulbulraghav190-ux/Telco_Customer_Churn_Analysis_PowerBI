# Telco Customer Churn Analysis – Power BI

An end-to-end Power BI project focused on analyzing customer churn, identifying high-risk customer segments, and generating actionable business insights for customer retention.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes telecom customer data to understand churn patterns across customer demographics, tenure, contract types, payment methods, and internet services.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of customer behavior and highlights the segments where churn is higher.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall customer churn and retention
- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Analyze churn by gender, partner, and dependents
- Understand churn across contract types
- Compare churn by payment methods
- Analyze churn by internet service
- Study customer tenure distribution
- Provide business recommendations for customer retention

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Overall customer churn rate is **26.54%**.
- Month-to-month customers show higher churn.
- Electronic check users show higher churn.
- Fiber optic customers show higher churn.
- Customer tenure is an important factor in understanding churn.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Target high-risk customers with focused retention offers.
- Encourage customers to choose longer-term contracts.
- Investigate service-related issues among fiber optic customers.
- Promote convenient alternative payment methods.
- Strengthen onboarding and support for newer customers.
